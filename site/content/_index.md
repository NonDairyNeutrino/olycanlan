# 🍁 Welcome to OlyCanLan

The **Olympia Canadian Highlander League** is a local, Discord-run league for the [Canadian Highlander](/canadian-highlander/) format of Magic: The Gathering. Matches are scheduled every other week, results are reported through our Discord bot, and everything you see here is generated straight from that live league data.
