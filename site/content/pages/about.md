## What is OlyCanLan?

The Olympia Canadian Highlander League (OlyCanLan) is a casual, community-run Magic: The Gathering league based around the [Canadian Highlander](/canadian-highlander/) format. We run seasons of paired rounds every other Monday, with results tracked and announced automatically by our Discord bot.

## League Roles

- **Battler ⚔️** — Competes in ranked rounds each season, submits a decklist, and is eligible for standings, points, and bounties.
- **Jammer 👊** — Joins casually to play games without competing in the ranked ladder. Great for newer players or anyone who wants in on the community without the commitment of weekly pairings.

## How a Season Works

1. **Signups open** — Players choose to join as a Battler or a Jammer using the bot's `/signup` commands.
2. **Decklists submitted** — Battlers submit a decklist for organizer review before the season begins.
3. **Rounds begin** — Every other Monday, new pairings are generated based on current standings (Swiss-style: similar records get paired together) and posted to the server.
4. **Matches reported** — Players report their own results using `/result`, which updates standings and, for bounty matches, posts the result publicly.
5. **Season ends** — Once someone finishes the season undefeated (or the round limit is reached), final standings are locked in and a new season begins.

See the [Standings](/standings/) page for what's happening right now, or [Player Records](/records/) for how everyone stacks up across every season we've run.

## The Bot

Everything on this site — standings, decklists, match history, and player records — is generated from the same data our Discord bot (`scorekeeper`) uses to run the league day to day. Check the [Bot Commands](/commands/) page for what you can do yourself in Discord.
